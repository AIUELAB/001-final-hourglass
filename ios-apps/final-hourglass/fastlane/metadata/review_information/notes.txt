Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for your feedback on version 1.0.7. We have made significant improvements to address the 4.2 guideline concerns. Here is a summary of the changes in v1.0.8:

1. HEALTH DASHBOARD (New Tab)
   - Added a dedicated "Health" tab (2nd tab, heart clipboard icon)
   - Users can self-evaluate 6 health factors: Sleep, Exercise, Diet, Stress, Alcohol, and Smoking
   - Each factor is scored on a 10-point scale with personalized improvement advice
   - Provides an overall health score with category-level breakdown

2. HEALTH SUMMARY CARD (Main Screen)
   - Added a health score summary card on the main TimeLimit screen
   - Displays the overall health score at a glance
   - Tapping navigates to the full Health Dashboard

3. BUNDLED EPISODES (Offline Content)
   - 41 curated biographical episodes are now bundled within the app
   - Content is immediately available without network connectivity
   - Episodes feature real historical figures' life milestones matched to the user's current age

4. IMPROVED EMPTY STATES
   - Added usage guides for first-time users in the Favorites tab
   - Clear call-to-action explaining how to bookmark episodes
   - Guides users through the core functionality

5. STREAMLINED FIRST LAUNCH
   - Added a "Skip" button to the initial hourglass animation
   - Users can immediately access all app features without waiting

HOW TO TEST:
- Launch the app and complete (or skip) the onboarding
- Tap the "Health" tab (2nd tab, heart clipboard icon)
- Enter health scores for each of the 6 factors
- View the overall health score and improvement advice
- Return to the TimeLimit tab to see the Health Summary Card
- Check the Favorites tab to see the usage guide (empty state)
- Browse the bundled episodes (no network required)
