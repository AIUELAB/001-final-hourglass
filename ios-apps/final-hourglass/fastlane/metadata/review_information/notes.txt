Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for your feedback on version 1.0.7. We have made significant improvements to address every concern raised under Guideline 4.2. Below is a detailed summary of v1.0.8 changes with concrete examples and step-by-step testing instructions.

========================================
WHAT CHANGED (Before v1.0.7 vs After v1.0.8)
========================================

| Area               | Before (v1.0.7)              | After (v1.0.8)                          |
|--------------------|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Health Dashboard   | Not present                  | Full 6-factor health scoring + advice   |
| Health on Main     | Not present                  | Summary card with overall score         |

========================================
1. HEALTH DASHBOARD (New "Health" Tab)
========================================
- Located as the 2nd tab (heart clipboard icon)
- Users self-evaluate 6 health factors on a 10-point scale:
  Sleep, Exercise, Diet, Stress, Alcohol, Smoking

CONCRETE ADVICE EXAMPLES:
  - Sleep score 3/10 → "Aim for 7-9 hours. Avoid screens 1 hour before bed."
  - Exercise score 2/10 → "Start with 15-minute walks. WHO recommends 150 min/week."
  - Stress score 4/10 → "Try the 4-7-8 breathing technique for immediate relief."
  - Each factor produces tailored advice based on the score range (1-3, 4-6, 7-10)

- Overall health score is calculated as a weighted average across all 6 factors
- Score categories: Excellent (80-100), Good (60-79), Fair (40-59), Needs Improvement (0-39)

========================================
2. HEALTH SUMMARY CARD (Main Screen)
========================================
- Displays the overall health score prominently on the TimeLimit screen
- Tapping the card navigates directly to the full Health Dashboard
- Provides at-a-glance health awareness without leaving the main screen

========================================
3. BUNDLED EPISODES (41 Offline Episodes)
========================================
- 41 curated biographical episodes are embedded in the app bundle
- Content is 100% available offline (enable Airplane Mode if desired to verify)
- AGE-MATCHING EXAMPLES:
  - User age 35 → sees "Steve Jobs at 35: founded NeXT after leaving Apple"
  - User age 26 → sees "Albert Einstein at 26: published the theory of special relativity"
  - User age 44 → sees "Marie Curie at 44: awarded second Nobel Prize in Chemistry"
- Episodes highlight real historical turning points at the user's current age
- Users can bookmark favorites for quick access

========================================
4. IMPROVED EMPTY STATES
========================================
- Favorites tab shows a friendly usage guide when no bookmarks exist
- Clear call-to-action: "Tap the heart icon on any episode to save it here"
- Guides first-time users through the bookmark workflow

========================================
5. STREAMLINED FIRST LAUNCH
========================================
- "Skip" button added to the initial hourglass animation
- Users can immediately access all features without waiting

========================================
HOW TO TEST (4-Step Walkthrough)
========================================

STEP 1 — Setup & First Launch
  1. Launch the app fresh (or delete and reinstall)
  2. Notice the "Skip" button on the hourglass animation
  3. Tap Skip to proceed immediately
  4. Set your birth date and profile information

STEP 2 — Health Dashboard
  1. Tap the "Health" tab (2nd tab, heart clipboard icon)
  2. Enter scores for all 6 health factors (e.g., Sleep=3, Exercise=5, Diet=7, Stress=4, Alcohol=8, Smoking=10)
  3. Observe personalized advice for each factor (e.g., Sleep=3 shows "Aim for 7-9 hours...")
  4. Check the overall health score and category at the top
  5. Return to the TimeLimit tab — the Health Summary Card now shows your score

STEP 3 — Episode Browsing & Age Matching
  1. Tap the Episodes tab (3rd tab)
  2. Browse the 41 bundled episodes — no loading spinner, instant display
  3. Note that episodes are matched to your profile age
  4. (Optional) Enable Airplane Mode to confirm fully offline functionality
  5. Tap the heart icon on any episode to bookmark it

STEP 4 — Favorites & Bookmarks
  1. Tap the Favorites tab (4th tab)
  2. If no bookmarks: see the usage guide with instructions
  3. If bookmarked: see your saved episodes listed
  4. Tap any saved episode to view its full content
